Strategic portfolio-steering platform — International University of Rabat

A dispositif to pilot the university's strategy: one platform tracking strategic axes, their projects, and the performance indicators that measure them.

The university's strategy lived across scattered documents — strategic axes in one place, project follow-ups in another, KPIs in spreadsheets — with no unified view for leadership to actually steer. I designed and implemented a portfolio-steering system that links each strategic axis to the projects delivering it and the indicators tracking its performance, giving real-time visibility from the ground up to the executive level.

I led it end to end under PMP® standards — gathering requirements with university leadership, shaping the data model, building the dashboards, and rolling it out as the reference tool for steering committees, governance, and activity reporting. It turned strategy from a static document into a living, measurable, pilotable system.

Research

Publication

Contemporary Management Research · 2025 · Open Access

Examining the Impact of Influencer Attributes on Consumer Trust and Purchase Intention: A PLS Path Modeling Approach

Kerkri, A.; Madani, M.A.; Dani, M.; Mdarhri, Y. — Engineering Sciences Laboratory (LSI), ENSA, Mohammed Premier University, Oujda

A study of how the perceived authenticity, attractiveness, and informativeness of Moroccan social-media influencers shape consumer trust and purchase intention — with trust as the mediating factor. Using PLS path modeling on 291 respondents plus k-means clustering, we found that attractiveness and informativeness drive trust (which in turn drives purchase intention), while perceived authenticity did not significantly affect trust.
