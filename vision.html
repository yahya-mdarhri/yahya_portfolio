What I'm looking for.

A quick filter, for both of us.

A strong fit

  • Roles. Data Project / Program Manager, Data Science Lead, Head of Data, AI Product Manager.
  • Contexts. Universities, ministries and public institutions, research organizations, foundations, and scale-ups, in Morocco, across MENA and Africa, or fully remote.
  • Access. A mandate that reaches leadership directly, not through several layers of relay.

How I work

A few things that don't change.

01

Delivery is part of the job, not a handoff.

A model or platform that isn't adopted hasn't solved anything. I stay through rollout, not just design.

02

If a number can't change a decision, it doesn't go on the dashboard.

Fewer metrics, used consistently, beat a long list nobody opens.

03

Arabic-language NLP needs more open resources.

Where the work isn't confidential, I publish it: models included, not just results.

04

An organization without legacy systems can build it right the first time.

Less to migrate, and no political weight from a past failed platform.
