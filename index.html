A.03 Tamkin · 2023–2026 label-tamkin.com ↗

NLP, LLMs and decision models for a national label

PROBLEM

Thousands of expert comments on city evaluations, far too many to read one by one.

WHAT I DID
  • Analysed 3,000+ expert verbatims with NLP to surface national priorities
  • Built a RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) pipeline so LLMs analyse documents and extract the information each evaluation needs
  • Built a roadmap module that ranks actions by gain, cost and horizon
  • Added real-time simulation of the label level each city can reach
RESULT

Deployed in 8 pilot cities, from Tangier to Dakhla
