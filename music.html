The project

Oriental songs → sheet music

A SaaS I built to solve a problem every oud student knows: the songs we love mostly live in recordings and oral tradition — not on paper. So I made a tool that listens, and writes it down.

◆ Music-tech · my own build

From a melody you hum
to a partition you can play.

Oriental music is famously hard to notate — the maqam system uses microtones that standard Western notation glosses over. My SaaS takes an oriental song and transcribes it into readable musical notation, so learners can actually sit down with their oud and play it, instead of rewinding a recording a hundred times by ear.

It's the clearest expression of who I am: a musician's problem, solved with a data scientist's tools.
